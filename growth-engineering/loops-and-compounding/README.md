# Loops and Compounding

The mental model the rest of the course runs on. Six lessons on why the funnel is the wrong
default picture, what the four loop types actually reinvest, how to compute your loop factor from
events you already have, whether speed or strength is worth more, why nearly every loop stalls,
and how to get from a vague story about word of mouth to a loop you can forecast.

| # | Lesson | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Funnels Leak, Loops Compound](01-funnels-leak-loops-compound.md) | What does a loop have that a funnel does not, and what is a loop below 1.0 worth? |
| 02 | [The Four Loop Types](02-the-four-loop-types.md) | Viral, content, paid and sales: what does each one reinvest, and what stops each one? |
| 03 | [Reading a Loop](03-reading-a-loop.md) | How do you compute your loop factor, and why do three honest methods disagree? |
| 04 | [Loop Speed and Loop Strength](04-loop-speed-and-loop-strength.md) | Which is worth more, a faster loop or a stronger one, and where is the crossover? |
| 05 | [Why Most Loops Die](05-why-most-loops-die.md) | What actually kills a loop, and how early can you see it coming? |
| 06 | [Drawing Your Own Loop](06-drawing-your-own-loop.md) | How do you turn a story about word of mouth into something you can forecast? |

Read these in order, and read lesson 01 even if loops are familiar, because the arithmetic of a
loop that cannot sustain itself is the part most treatments skip and it is the case nearly every
company is actually in.

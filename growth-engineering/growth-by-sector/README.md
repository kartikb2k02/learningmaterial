# Growth by Sector

The same loops, bent by the market they run in. Seven sectors, each a delta rather than a
restatement: what the market's shape does to the channel list, which techniques invert, where the
real constraint sits, and the arithmetic that settles it. Each lesson links to its counterpart in
[Product by Sector](../../product-management/product-by-sector/README.md) for the buyer, the cycle
and the regulation, and never repeats them.

| # | Lesson | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Enterprise B2B SaaS](01-enterprise-b2b-saas.md) | What does a countable market do to every growth technique? |
| 02 | [Fintech](02-fintech.md) | Which costs does a fintech funnel carry that no channel report shows? |
| 03 | [Ecommerce and D2C](03-ecommerce-and-d2c.md) | How much of an order survives to pay for acquiring it? |
| 04 | [Marketplaces](04-marketplaces.md) | Why does spending on one side destroy the other? |
| 05 | [Devtools and Infrastructure](05-devtools-and-infrastructure.md) | Why is the documentation the funnel, and which techniques invert? |
| 06 | [Healthtech and Regulated Care](06-healthtech-and-regulated-care.md) | What changes when the calendar rather than the funnel is the constraint? |
| 07 | [Vertical SaaS](07-vertical-saas.md) | What decides a win when the market fits on a spreadsheet? |

**Read the product sector lesson first.** The shape of a product usually determines more than the
market it sells into, and a fintech marketplace behaves like a marketplace that happens to move
money. Borrowing the fintech growth playbook for it will mislead you about liquidity, which is the
thing that will actually kill it.

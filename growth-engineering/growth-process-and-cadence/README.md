belongs on a weekly agenda and what the agenda costs in decisions, where the pipeline jams and why
widening the wrong stage does nothing, what a score gap can actually tell you, what an archive is
worth once the idea space starts running out, who owns a contested surface, and what the residue of
a year of experimenting costs the next year.

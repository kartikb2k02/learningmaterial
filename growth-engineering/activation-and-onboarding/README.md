# Activation and Onboarding

The first product surface growth owns outright. What activation has to mean for your product
rather than somebody else's, how to tell an aha moment from a description of intent, which minute
of the flow is actually expensive, when asking for more raises the numbers, what the first screen
should show somebody with no data, why tours die with their length, and why this is the hardest
thing in the product to test honestly.

# Product Led Growth

Whether a product can be sold without a salesperson is two arithmetic questions, and most arguments
about PLG are disagreements about which one the product fails. This topic covers both gates, the
routing rule that decides which signups get a human, which product signals are worth somebody's
hour, how adoption spreads and stalls inside one company, the honest disqualifiers, and what sits
above the ceiling a self serve motion can reach.

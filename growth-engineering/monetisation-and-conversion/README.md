# Monetisation and Conversion

Where the decision to pay is actually made, which is rarely where the conversion work happens.
What each way of giving the product away selects for and what it costs to run, what a paywall
teaches the people it does not convert, the moments worth asking at and when to stop adding them,
how an account grows without a salesperson, and what a fair save looks like.

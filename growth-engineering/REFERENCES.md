# References

Where to go for more, organised by the part of the job each source serves. Every link here returned
a live page when checked on **28 October 2026**. Anything that could not be reached from a plain
request was left out rather than quoted from memory, which is why some well known names are missing.

These are not a reading list to work through. They are places to go when a specific lesson raised a
question this course deliberately stopped short of answering, and the right time to open one is when
you have a decision in front of you.

## The function and the loops

| Source | What it is good for |
|---|---|
| [Brian Balfour's essays](https://brianbalfour.com/essays) | Market, product, channel and model fit as a single system. The clearest writing on why a channel works for one product and not another |
| [Andrew Chen](https://andrewchen.com/) | Network effects, cold start problems and the mechanics of consumer loops |
| [Reforge blog](https://www.reforge.com/blog) | Working practitioner material on loops, models and growth team structure |
| [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/) | Interview driven and broad, strongest on how teams actually operate |
| [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/) | Kyle Poyar on product led growth benchmarks and go to market motion |
| [NFX](https://www.nfx.com/) | Network effects taxonomy and marketplace dynamics, with more rigour than most |

## What is deliberately not here

Case studies of famous growth results, because the ones that circulate are selected for being
surprising rather than for being representative, and the arithmetic in
[What Good Looks Like](the-growth-function/06-what-good-looks-like.md) explains why most of them
are noise.

Benchmark collections presented without a denominator. A conversion rate with no definition of the
population is not a benchmark, and comparing against one is the error in
[The Denominator Problem](../product-management/how-to-read-a-metric/02-the-denominator-problem.md).

Vendor material that is a product description wearing a methodology costume. Several of the sources
above are published by vendors and earn their place by being useful about the mechanism rather than
about the product.

# Paid Acquisition

The channel with the fastest feedback and the most ways to be confidently wrong. Six lessons on
what the money actually buys, how the price is set and what you control in it, why targeting
moved into the creative, where a paid click really gets lost, which columns of a paid report
mislead, and the arithmetic that decides whether paid compounds or merely runs while the money
does.

# Virality and Referral

The channel everybody wants and few measure honestly. Six lessons on what the loop factor hides
about who is actually doing the inviting, why most referral programmes produce almost nothing,
what makes a collaboration loop different from an incentive, how to design an invitation that
gets accepted, how to split a reward between the two people involved, and the fraud that arrives
in exact proportion to what you pay.

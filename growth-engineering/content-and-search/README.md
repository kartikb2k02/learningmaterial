# Content and Search

The slowest channel and the one with the highest ceiling. Six lessons on what actually decides
what gets shown, why search volume ranks your keywords in nearly the opposite order to revenue,
when generating pages at scale helps and when it costs you the pages you already had, the
difference between a library that compounds and one that evaporates, how authority is earned
without the practices that get sites penalised, and what changes when the answer is written
rather than linked.

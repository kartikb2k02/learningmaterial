# Engagement and Resurrection

How often a product should be used is decided by the job it does, not by your target. This topic
covers the ceiling that fact imposes, how a habit hands over from your prompts to the user's own
cues, where the line sits between a habit and a dark pattern, which churn signals arrive early
enough to act on, what a dormant user is still worth, and how to decide to stay quiet.

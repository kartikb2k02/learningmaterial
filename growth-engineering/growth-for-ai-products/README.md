# Growth for AI Products

One new term in the cost structure, a cost per use, and the growth curve changes shape. These six
lessons work through the consequences: a free tier that scales as a liability, activation that
depends on getting a second attempt, retention that breaks when the model changes, a viral loop
that decays on its own, and a margin that falls fastest in your best cohort.

| # | Lesson | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Why AI Products Grow Differently](01-why-ai-products-grow-differently.md) | What is structurally different about the curve? |
| 02 | [Free Tiers When Every Use Costs Money](02-free-tiers-when-every-use-costs-money.md) | How much can you give away, and to whom? |
| 03 | [Activation When the First Output Is Bad](03-activation-when-the-first-output-is-bad.md) | How do you get a second attempt out of somebody? |
| 04 | [Retention When the Model Changes](04-retention-when-the-model-changes.md) | What happens to habit when the product silently changes under the user? |
| 05 | [The Demo Loop](05-the-demo-loop.md) | Why does sharing output work as a channel, and when does it stop? |
| 06 | [Usage Growth That Destroys Margin](06-usage-growth-that-destroys-margin.md) | What do you do when your best cohort is your most expensive? |

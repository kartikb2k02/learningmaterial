# Acquisition Channels

The level starts here because every channel lesson that follows assumes these six. What decides
which channels exist for your product, how to test one without wasting the budget, when a channel
is genuinely finished rather than badly run, what to do the week one dies, and why no two
attribution reports will ever agree.

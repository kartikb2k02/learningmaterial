# Evaluation and Quality

The topic the rest of this course rests on. An evaluation is to an AI product what a test suite is
to ordinary software: the thing that lets you change a prompt, a model or a retrieval setting and
know whether you made it better or worse. Teams without one ship on intuition, and intuition does
not notice a four point regression.

# Model Lifecycle and Drift

Your product depends on something you do not control and that changes without asking. A model
upgrade is a breaking change delivered by a third party, and the discipline for surviving it is the
same discipline that lets you improve anything safely, which is why this topic sits next to
evaluation rather than in operations.
